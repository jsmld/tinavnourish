steps:
  - Heat the oven to 170°C (340°F) and line a 900g (2lb) loaf tin with baking paper.
  - Mash the bananas in a large bowl, then whisk in the sugar, oil, eggs and vanilla until smooth.
  - Sift the flour, baking powder, cinnamon and bicarb straight into the bowl and fold until just combined.
  - Toss the blueberries in a teaspoon of flour (this stops them sinking), then fold them through the batter.
  - Scrape into the tin and bake for 50–55 minutes, until a skewer comes out with moist crumbs but no wet batter.
  - Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack to cool fully before slicing.

The trick to a deeply flavoured banana loaf is the bananas themselves — let them go fully black on the counter before you bake. Their sugars concentrate and the loaf tastes noticeably richer for it.
