steps:
  - Keep the stock hot in a small pan on a back burner.
  - Sweat the onion in olive oil over medium heat for 6–8 minutes until translucent, then add the garlic and rice.
  - Toast the rice for 1 minute, then pour in the wine and stir until absorbed.
  - Add the hot stock a ladle at a time, stirring often, for about 18 minutes or until the rice is al dente.
  - Stir through the peas in the last 2 minutes of cooking.
  - Off the heat, beat in the lemon juice, zest, parmesan and a generous grind of pepper. Rest 1 minute before serving.

Risotto is a weeknight dinner masquerading as a project. Keep the stock hot, stir when you think of it, and don't be precious about the wine — whatever you'd drink with it is perfect.
