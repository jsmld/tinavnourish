steps:
  - Score the cut sides of the aubergine halves in a tight diamond pattern, taking care not to cut through the skin.
  - Heat the grill (broiler) to high and brush the cut sides with oil. Grill for 8–10 minutes until deeply golden and soft.
  - Whisk the miso, mirin, soy, sugar and ginger in a small bowl until smooth.
  - Spoon the glaze generously over the scored flesh and return to the grill for 4–5 minutes until bubbling and charred at the edges.
  - Rest for 2 minutes, then scatter with sesame seeds and sliced spring onion.
  - Serve with short-grain rice and a crunchy cucumber salad.
