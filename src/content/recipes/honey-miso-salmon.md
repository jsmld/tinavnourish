steps:
  - Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Whisk the miso, honey, soy, ginger and sesame oil until smooth.
  - Toss the bok choy with the neutral oil and a pinch of salt and spread on a lined baking tray.
  - Place the salmon fillets on top, skin-side down, and brush generously with the miso glaze.
  - Roast for 12–14 minutes until the salmon flakes easily and the glaze is lacquered and bubbling.
  - Finish under a hot grill for 1–2 minutes if you want the top extra-tacky.
  - Serve over rice with the bok choy and spring onion, spooning any tray juices over the top.

The honey-miso glaze does triple duty here: it lacquers the salmon, sweetens the bok choy underneath, and turns the tray juices into something you'll want to spoon over rice. One tray, fifteen minutes.
