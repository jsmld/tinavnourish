steps:
  - Warm the ghee in a heavy pan over medium heat and sweat the onion for 6 minutes until soft.
  - Add the spices and stir for 30 seconds until toasted and fragrant.
  - Tip in the carrots and lentils and stir to coat, then pour in the stock.
  - Simmer for 20–25 minutes until the carrots are completely tender and the lentils have broken down.
  - Blend with a stick blender until completely smooth, then season well with salt and lemon juice to taste.
  - Serve in wide bowls with a swirl of yoghurt, a spoonful of crisped shallots and torn coriander.

The kind of soup that gets you through February. Red lentils disappear entirely into the broth — they're here for body and protein, not texture. Don't skip the final blend; the silkiness is the whole point.
