steps:
  - Melt the coconut oil in a wide pan over medium heat and sweat the onion for 8 minutes until soft and translucent.
  - Add the garlic, ginger, curry powder, turmeric and chilli flakes and cook for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
  - Tip in the chickpeas and stir to coat in the spices.
  - Pour in the coconut milk and chopped tomatoes, season with salt, and simmer uncovered for 20 minutes until thickened.
  - Stir through the spinach in the last minute, then finish with lemon juice and taste for seasoning.
  - Serve over rice or with flatbread, scattered with coriander.

The kind of curry you can make on a Tuesday from things already in your cupboard. It reheats brilliantly, so make a double batch and freeze half — the flavours actually deepen overnight.
