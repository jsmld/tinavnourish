steps:
  - Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Spread the tomatoes cut-side up on a tray with the shallots and garlic.
  - Drizzle with olive oil, season with salt, and roast for 40–45 minutes until the tomatoes are collapsing and lightly charred at the edges.
  - Squeeze the soft garlic cloves from their skins and add to a blender with the tomatoes, shallots and any pan juices.
  - Add the tomato purée, smoked paprika, coconut cream and hot stock, then blend until completely smooth.
  - Taste and adjust seasoning — it will likely need more salt than you think.
  - Warm through gently on the stove and serve with basil and good bread.

This is the soup that converted every "I don't like tomato soup" person in my life. The slow roast concentrates everything that's good about a summer tomato, and the coconut cream adds richness without dairy.
