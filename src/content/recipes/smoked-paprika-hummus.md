steps:
  - Rub the chickpeas between two clean tea towels to remove the skins — this is the secret to a genuinely smooth hummus.
  - Blend the tahini, lemon juice, garlic and cumin in a food processor for 1 minute until pale and thick.
  - With the motor running, stream in the ice-cold water and blend another minute until the mixture is light and fluffy.
  - Add the skinned chickpeas and 4 tablespoons of olive oil, then blend for a full 3 minutes, scraping down twice.
  - If the hummus feels tight, loosen with a tablespoon of the reserved chickpea liquid at a time until it's silken.
  - Spread on a wide plate, swirl with the back of a spoon, and finish with smoked paprika, toasted pine nuts and a generous glug of olive oil.

Peeling chickpeas sounds like the kind of fuss only restaurant cooks bother with, but it makes a difference you can taste. Ten minutes of work, an entirely different texture. Save a few unskinned ones for garnish if you like the look.
