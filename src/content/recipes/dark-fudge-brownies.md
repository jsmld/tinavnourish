steps:
  - Heat the oven to 170°C (340°F) and line a 20cm square tin with baking paper.
  - Melt the chocolate and butter together in a heatproof bowl over simmering water, then set aside to cool for 5 minutes.
  - Whisk in the sugar until dissolved, then beat in the eggs one at a time until the mixture is glossy.
  - Fold in the ground almonds, cocoa powder, vanilla and a pinch of salt until just combined — do not overmix.
  - Pour the batter into the tin and bake for 28–30 minutes, until the top is crackled and the centre is just set.
  - Cool completely in the tin, then cut into 9 squares and finish with flaky sea salt.

These brownies were the recipe that started this whole site. They are intentionally dense — closer to a truffle than a cake — and the ground almonds keep them tender and naturally gluten-free. Don't skip the flaky salt on top; it's the whole personality.
