steps:
  - Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F). Toss the broccoli with 2 tablespoons of olive oil and a generous pinch of salt.
  - Spread on a baking sheet and roast for 10–12 minutes until the florets are deeply charred at the edges but still have bite.
  - Whisk the tahini, lemon juice and zest, garlic and the remaining olive oil with 2 tablespoons of water until smooth and pourable.
  - Pile the broccoli onto a platter and drizzle over the tahini dressing.
  - Finish with the toasted almonds, aleppo pepper and flaky salt. Serve warm or at room temperature.

The trick to charred broccoli is high heat and not crowding the tray — you want blackened edges, not steamed middles. The tahini dressing is robust enough to stand up to the bitterness of the char; don't be delicate with it.
