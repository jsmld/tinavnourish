steps:
  - The evening before baking, mix the starter, both flours, water and salt in a bowl until no dry spots remain. Cover and rest at room temperature for 30 minutes.
  - Perform four sets of stretch-and-folds at 30-minute intervals, until the dough feels strong and stretchy.
  - Bulk-ferment at room temperature for 3–4 hours until the dough is puffy and about 50% risen, then shape into a tight round.
  - Dust a proving basket generously with rye flour, place the dough seam-side up, and refrigerate overnight (12–16 hours).
  - The next morning, heat the oven to its hottest setting with a heavy lidded pot inside for 45 minutes.
  - Turn the dough onto parchment, score the top, lower into the pot, cover, and bake 25 minutes covered then 20 minutes uncovered until the crust is deep and the loaf sounds hollow.

Rye dough behaves differently to wheat — it's sticky, it's heavy, and it won't pass a windowpane test the way a white loaf will. Don't fight it. The reward is a dense, moist, slightly sweet loaf that keeps for a week and toasts beautifully.
