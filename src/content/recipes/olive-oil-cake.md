steps:
  - Heat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Brush a 23cm round cake tin with olive oil and line the base with baking paper.
  - Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt in a medium bowl.
  - In a large bowl, beat the eggs and sugar on high speed until pale and tripled in volume, about 4 minutes.
  - Whisk in the milk, lemon zest and the 250ml olive oil until emulsified.
  - Fold in the dry ingredients until just combined, then scrape into the tin and bake for 35–40 minutes until a skewer comes out clean.
  - Cool for 15 minutes, then turn out. Whisk the lemon juice into the icing sugar to make a thick glaze and pour over the cooled cake.

An olive oil cake should taste like olives, not neutral oil — use the peppery, fruity stuff you'd dress a tomato with. The glaze is intentionally sharp; it cuts through the richness of the cake and keeps the whole thing from reading as sweet.
