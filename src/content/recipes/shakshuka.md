steps:
  - Heat the olive oil in a wide cast-iron pan over medium heat and sweat the onion for 6 minutes.
  - Add the capsicums and cook for 8–10 minutes until very soft and beginning to caramelise at the edges.
  - Stir in the garlic, cumin, paprika and chilli and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  - Tip in the tomatoes with a generous pinch of salt and simmer for 10 minutes until thickened and jammy.
  - Make four wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook 5–7 minutes until the whites are set but the yolks still wobble.
  - Scatter over the feta and herbs and serve straight from the pan with crusty bread.

Shakshuka is the rare dish that feels equally right at 10am on a Sunday and 9pm on a Wednesday. The key is reducing the tomato sauce until it's almost jammy before the eggs go in — wet sauce means sad whites.
